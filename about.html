FALLD

FALLD is a small retro arcade game about one simple thing: falling. You drop down a narrow shaft that's never the same twice — every run generates a brand new layout, so there's no pattern to memorize, only reflexes to sharpen.

How to play

One button, one move: tap (or click) to jump to the opposite wall as you fall. Time it right to dodge the spikes lining the shaft, and pay attention — some of them start swinging the deeper you go.

  • Coins sit on the far wall as a small detour, a risk-reward call every time.
  • Bats and rats start showing up past 300 meters — they don't forgive a bad jump.
  • A gun pickup appears around the same depth, clearing nearby threats automatically for a few seconds.

Depth is the only score that matters. Falling as far as possible — measured in meters — is the whole game.

Why it exists

FALLD started as a weekend project: a love letter to old-school one-button arcade games (think Flappy Bird's directness, with a vertical twist) built with plain HTML5 canvas, no game engine, no downloads. It's meant to be picked up and put down in thirty seconds, on any phone or laptop.
